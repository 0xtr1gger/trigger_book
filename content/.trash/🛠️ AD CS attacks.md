## AD CS attacks

>[!abstract] Active Directory Certificate Services (AD CS) attacks abuse a misconfigured enterprise PKI. Certificates are a durable authentication credential: a stolen or maliciously issued certificate can be used to obtain Kerberos tickets as its subject, so certificate-based primitives enable privilege escalation, lateral movement, and persistence that survives password resets.
