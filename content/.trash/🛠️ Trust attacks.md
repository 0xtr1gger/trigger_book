## Trust attacks

>[!abstract] Domain and forest trusts let principals in one domain authenticate to resources in another. Trust attacks abuse that relationship to move beyond the current domain — escalating from a child domain to its forest root, or pivoting across a trust into a separate forest — typically by forging tickets that carry privileged SIDs the trust will honour.
